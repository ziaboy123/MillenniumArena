Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026
Draft · to be reviewed before launch

Millennium Arena is a free fan project. It has no ads, no trackers, and never sells or shares your data. This page explains what it keeps and why, in plain English.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, username, and your password (stored only as a secure hash, never the password itself).
  • What you build and play: your decks, settings, match history and stats.
  • Chat: messages sent during duels, kept for 30 days so reports of abuse can be checked, then deleted.
  • Security: the devices you've chosen to trust, so we know when to ask for a login code.

How we use your email

Only for verification codes, password resets and important account notices. No newsletters and no marketing.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you logged in. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Who can see what

Your username, rank, stats and match history appear on your public profile. Your email and decks stay private unless you choose to share a deck publicly.

Your rights

You can download or delete your data at any time. Deleting your account in Settings removes everything above. For anything else, email daniyal@daniyalzia.co.uk.
